About BM DESIGN

Designing spaces that shape the future and bring dreams to life.

Who We Are

BM DESIGN is an innovative architecture and interior design studio dedicated to crafting meaningful, functional, and aesthetically refined spaces. Based in Tamil Nadu, we bring creative solutions that merge modern design principles with the personal dreams of every client. Our philosophy revolves around thoughtful planning, sustainable approaches, and unique concepts that help you visualize— and live—your future.

Founder & Vision

Ar. Sathishkumar Baskaran, B.Arch, M.A — the visionary behind BM DESIGN — completed his Bachelor's in Architecture in 2020 and went on to pursue a Master's in Interior Architecture and Design in the United Kingdom, graduating in 2023. His global perspective, combined with deep-rooted design sensibilities, drives the studio’s commitment to excellence.

Under his guidance, BM DESIGN continues to evolve as a brand that believes architecture is more than construction — it is an experience. A journey. A connection. We help you see the future before it is built. Whether it’s your dream home, a commercial space, or a complete interior transformation, we design with purpose, passion, and precision.
