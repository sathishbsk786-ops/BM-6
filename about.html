About BM DESIGN

Crafting timeless spaces that tell your story.

Who We Are

BM DESIGN is an architectural and interior design studio built on the belief that great design can transform the way people live, feel, and experience their surroundings. Our philosophy blends modern aesthetics with functionality — creating thoughtful spaces that balance beauty, purpose, and comfort.

Our Founder

Ar. Sathishkumar Baskaran, B.Arch, M.A — Founder Architect of BM DESIGN — completed his Bachelor’s in Architecture in 2020 and earned his Master’s degree in Interior Architecture and Design in the United Kingdom in 2023. His international exposure, technical expertise, and passion for meaningful design define the identity of BM DESIGN.

Our Vision

At BM DESIGN, we help you see the future before it is built. We believe architecture is more than creating structures — it is a journey of imagination, emotion, and personal expression. Every project we design carries depth, character, and clarity to enhance the way people live and interact with spaces.

Designing Your Dream House

A dream home is more than bricks and walls — it is a reflection of who you are. Our process focuses on understanding your lifestyle, dreams, and aesthetic preferences to create a home that feels truly meaningful. Every detail is crafted with intention, ensuring your home is both beautiful and deeply personal.

With BM DESIGN, your home becomes a vision brought to life — a place where your future takes shape with purpose and style.
