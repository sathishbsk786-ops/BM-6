About BM DESIGN

Crafting timeless spaces that tell your story.

Who We Are

BM DESIGN is an architectural and interior design studio built on the belief that great design can transform the way people live, feel, and experience their surroundings. Our philosophy blends modern aesthetics with practical function — bringing creativity and purpose into every space we create.

Our Founder

Ar. Sathishkumar Baskaran, B.Arch, M.A — Founder Architect of BM DESIGN — completed his Bachelor’s in Architecture in 2020 and earned his Master’s degree in Interior Architecture and Design in the UK in 2023. His global exposure, refined design sensibility, and passion for creative innovation guide the vision of our studio.

Our Vision

At BM DESIGN, we help you see the future before it is built. Every project is crafted with care, detail, and imagination. We don't just design structures — we create experiences. Whether it's your dream home, a commercial space, or a complete interior transformation, our designs are made to inspire, comfort, and elevate your lifestyle.

Designing Your Dream House

Your dream home is more than walls and rooms — it is a reflection of your identity. Our team works closely with you to understand your lifestyle, aspirations, and desires to craft a space that feels truly yours. From thoughtful planning to aesthetic detailing, every element is curated to give your home a unique personality.

With BM DESIGN, you don't just build a house — you create a future worth living in.
